4.4 Diagnosis Timing Relative to RNA-seq Acquisition

A diagnosis is counted by acquisition when identified in the same or an earlier wave. Age-18+ diagnoses use p1dawbaage or sdawbaage.

Exclusions

Pre-18-only cases and diagnoses first observed after acquisition are excluded from controls. Participants without an age-18+ evaluation fall outside the adult denominator.
